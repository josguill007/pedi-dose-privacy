Privacy Policy & Legal Terms

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 | Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy-First Commitment

Welcome to PediDose. Your privacy and the privacy of your children are our highest priority. PediDose is architected as an offline-first utility:

  • No Account Required: You never need to create an account, register, or enter personal contact details to use PediDose.
  • 100% On-Device Data Storage: Child profiles (names, dates of birth, weights, allergies) and dose logs are stored strictly on your local device. We never upload, sync, or transmit your personal data to remote servers.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not include third-party advertising or user behavioral tracking SDKs.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. Local Profile & Dose Data: Information entered into the application (e.g., child's weight, age, administered dosages) is stored exclusively in your device's private application sandbox using local preferences. This data remains on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app.

B. Public FDA API Searches: When using the search feature in the FDA Guide tab, the app sends search queries over encrypted HTTPS to the public OpenFDA API (operated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration). These requests contain only the generic or brand drug query and contain zero personal, child, or device identification data.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

PediDose is a utility designed for parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13. All data entered regarding children remains exclusively under the control of the parent on their physical device.

⚠️ Medical & Safety Disclaimer:
PediDose is an educational calculation aid and reference tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. Always inspect medication packaging, verify bottle concentrations, and consult your pediatrician or healthcare provider before administering medicine. In case of an emergency, call 911 or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) immediately.

4. Data Security

Because all data is retained locally on your mobile device, your data security is safeguarded by your operating system's built-in protections (passcode, biometric lock, hardware encryption). We recommend maintaining appropriate security on your device.
