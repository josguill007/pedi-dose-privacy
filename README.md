# Privacy Policy for PediDose

**Effective Date:** September 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 16, 2026

Welcome to **PediDose** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of you and your family. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PediDose mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Summary (Privacy First)

* **Zero Personal Data Collection**: PediDose does not require user accounts, email registration, or logins.
* **100% On-Device Storage**: All child profiles (names, dates of birth, weights, heights, allergies) and dose logs are stored **locally on your device** and are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud storage.
* **No Tracking or Advertising**: We do not use third-party advertising trackers, user profiling, or behavioral tracking SDKs.

---

### 2. Information Handled by the Application

#### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
When using the App, you may enter information to calculate dosages and track administration:
* Child profile details: Name, birth date (or age), weight, height, and allergy notes.
* Medication dose logs: Timestamps, medication name, dosage volume ($mL$), and dosage amount ($mg$).

> **All of this data is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (via secure sandboxed local preferences). If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this data will be deleted.**

#### B. Network Requests (OpenFDA API)
When you search for medication packaging or active ingredients in the *FDA Guide* tab:
* The App queries the public **OpenFDA API** (`api.fda.gov`) managed by the United States Food and Drug Administration.
* These network requests contain **only the search term (medication name)** over encrypted HTTPS.
* **No personal data, child profiles, device identifiers, or location data are ever included in OpenFDA requests.**

---

### 3. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

PediDose is designed as a tool for **parents, guardians, and adult caregivers**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All data entered regarding children remains strictly on the parent's device and is never uploaded, shared, or processed externally.

---

### 4. Data Security

Because all personal data resides exclusively on your local device, the security of your data is protected by your device's operating system security features (passcode, Face ID, fingerprint, device encryption). We recommend keeping your mobile device secured.

---

### 5. Medical and Health Disclaimer

* PediDose is an educational and mathematical calculation aid intended to assist parents and caregivers with pediatric medication guidelines.
* **PediDose does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**
* Always inspect medication packaging, verify bottle concentrations with the included oral syringe, and consult your pediatrician, healthcare provider, or pharmacist before administering any medication.
* In the event of a medical emergency, immediately contact your local emergency services (e.g., 911 in the United States) or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
